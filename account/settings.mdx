The **Settings** page is the home for everything that affects your account globally - your PMS connection, integration health, your timezone, billing, and (when needed) deleting the account.

<Frame caption="The Settings page with a Guesty Marketplace connection">
  ![Settings page](/images/screenshots/settings-overview.png)
</Frame>

The page is organized into cards:

1. **Guesty API Credentials**
2. **Integration Status**
3. **Account Preferences**
4. **Billing & Payment**
5. **Danger Zone**

## Guesty API Credentials

This card shows how your Guesty account is connected and lets you remove the connection. A **Connected via Guesty Marketplace** badge sits next to the card title, and the subtitle reads *Your Guesty account is connected and syncing properties*.

Guesty issued the credentials when you connected, so there is nothing to paste or edit here. See [Connect Guesty](/integrations/connect-guesty) for how the connection is made.

<Note>
  This card is provider-specific. On an OwnerRez account it reads **OwnerRez Connected** instead, with the subtitle *Your OwnerRez account is connected via OAuth* (or *via Personal Access Token*) and a **Disconnect** button. Before any PMS is connected, the card is **Connect Your PMS** and shows the provider picker.
</Note>

<Frame caption="Guesty API Credentials card, connected via the Guesty Marketplace">
  ![Guesty connected](/images/screenshots/settings-guesty-connected.png)
</Frame>

### What the card shows

- **Guesty Account ID** - a read-only identifier for your Guesty account. Quote this ID when you contact Guesty support. Your Guesty account name appears just below the field.
- **Connected** - the date the connection was last established.

<Note>
  If the Account ID field is empty and reads *Not read from Guesty yet. It appears after the next sync*, the connection predates this field. It fills in automatically after the next daily sync.
</Note>

To reconnect, disconnect PX Accounting on its [partner page in Guesty](https://app.guesty.com/integrations/partners/pxaccounting) and connect again. The connection is not editable from inside PX: Guesty issues the credentials in exchange for a one-time key and does not make them retrievable afterwards, so there is nothing here to change.

### Deleting the connection

Click **Delete** to remove the connection. PX asks you to confirm, then:

- Removes the stored credentials.
- Unregisters the webhook.
- **Removes all synced properties.**

<Warning>
  Deleting Guesty credentials also deletes your synced properties. This cannot be undone.
</Warning>

You can only have **one** active PMS at a time. Switching providers means deleting the existing connection first.

## Integration Status

This card reports the health of your PMS integration and lets you retry anything that failed. It shows up to two rows, depending on your provider and connection state.

**Admin Account** (Guesty only, and only when PX manages one) - reads *Created: `<email>`* with a green check, or *Not created yet* with a red X and a **Retry** button.

**Webhooks** - always shown. The summary line tells you exactly where you stand:

| Summary | Meaning | Retry available |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *Registered and active* | Healthy. Changes in your PMS reach PX as they happen. | - |
| *Registered to an old address - re-register to fix* | The webhook points at a stale URL. | Yes |
| *Partially registered (n/m)* | Only some webhook events registered. | Yes |
| *Not registered* | No webhook is registered. | Yes |
| *Not configured on server* | Marked N/A. Nothing to do on your side. | No |
| *Not available (requires OAuth)* | Marked N/A. Your connection type does not support webhooks. | No |
| *Status unknown* | PX could not determine the state. The error follows the label. | Yes |

Each failed row has its own **Retry** button - there is no page-wide refresh. The most common failure is a Guesty plan limit on the number of webhooks. Remove an unused webhook in Guesty, then click **Retry**.

## Account Preferences

A single setting:

- **Timezone** - Eastern (ET), Central (CT), Mountain (MT), Arizona (MST), Pacific (PT), Alaska (AKT), or Hawaii (HST). New accounts default to Central.

All date calculations for diagnostics and issue detection use this timezone. See [Timezone](/account/timezone) for what the setting affects and what it does not.

## Billing & Payment

Manage the payment method used for monthly subscription billing:

- **No payment method** - shows an **Add Payment Method** button that opens Stripe Checkout in a new tab.
- **Card on file** - shows the brand and last 4 (for example, *Visa ending in 4242*) plus expiration, with a **Remove** button.
- **Bank account on file** - shows the bank name and last 4, with a **Remove** button.

See [Billing](/account/billing) for the full billing workflow.

## Danger Zone

A single button: **Delete Account**, described as *Permanently delete your account and all associated data*.

Clicking it opens a confirmation dialog with a verification step. Deleting:

- Removes your PMS credentials.
- Removes business models, tax configuration, and audit history.
- Cancels billing.
- Logs you out.

Irreversible. See [Delete account](/account/delete-account).

## Layout notes

Settings is the **first stop** for new users. PX sends new sign-ins here when no PMS is configured, because nothing else works without a PMS connection.

Existing users typically only return to Settings to:

- Check integration status after a change on the PMS side.
- Update the timezone after moving offices.
- Replace a payment method.
- Disconnect when offboarding.
