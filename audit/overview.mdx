PX runs **two** audits. They answer different questions, produce different kinds of results, and you reach them in different places:

| | Financial Audit | Accounting Audit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Question it answers** | Were the right amounts charged? | Did the money and the books end up in the right place? |
| **Covers** | Fees, taxes, and commission | Ledger and guest-money integrity |
| **Scope you choose** | A date range, up to 62 days | A calendar month |
| **Result** | Findings per reservation, plus a verdict | Issues you work through and close out |
| **Severity scale** | `error`, `warning`, `info` | High, medium, low |
| **Where you run it** | **Financial Audit** in the sidebar | As part of **Month-End Close** |

Neither audit modifies your PMS. Both are read-only: PX audits the data your PMS produces.

## Which one do you want?

Reach for the **Financial Audit** when you have a question about amounts on bookings: a tax that looks wrong, a commission that does not match the agreement, a specific booking you want to investigate. You control the exact window, so it is the right tool for a narrow look at recent history or a single property.

Reach for the **Accounting Audit** when you are closing a period and need the books to hold up: payments that never landed, ledger entries that do not balance, cancellations that left money stranded. It is scoped to a month because that is the unit a close is measured in.

Most operators end up using [Month-End Close](/audit/month-end-close), which runs both over the same month and shows the combined exposure in one place. You do not have to wait for month end to use it - it runs on demand, any day of the month, for any month you pick.

## When to run an audit

- **Period close** - use [Month-End Close](/audit/month-end-close) for the month you are closing. It covers both audits at once.
- **Owner statement prep** - run before sending statements to catch issues that affect payouts.
- **After a configuration change** - changed a tax rate or commission percent? Run a Financial Audit over the affected period to verify the new rules are applied.
- **Onboarding a new owner or property** - audit recent history to validate the new business model.
- **Investigation** - when a specific booking looks wrong, run a Financial Audit over a narrow window with a listing filter.

## What the Financial Audit returns

A successful run returns three layers:

### Summary

- **Reservations Audited** - total bookings in scope.
- **Issues Found** - reservations with at least one finding.
- **Verdict** - one of `CLEAN`, `UNDERPAID`, `OVERPAID`. The verdict reflects the net financial direction.

### Per-reservation results

A table with one row per reservation:

- Confirmation code (linked to the PMS where applicable).
- Listing, guest, channel, status.
- Number of findings.
- Net discrepancy (positive when you under-collected, negative when you over-collected).

Click a row to expand it. The expansion shows the **line items** on the booking and the **findings**, each with severity, expected, actual, difference, source, and reasoning.

### Findings breakdown

A grid of finding types with counts. Click a type to filter the results table.

## Constraints

These apply to the Financial Audit. Month-End Close takes a month instead of a range, so it is not affected.

- The audit window cannot exceed **62 days**. For longer ranges, run multiple audits back to back.
- The Generate button is disabled when the range exceeds 62 days, the start date is after the end date, or you have not connected a PMS yet.

## How long does an audit take?

It depends on how many reservations are in the window. As a rough guide, PX checks **about 80 reservations per minute**, so:

- A small portfolio over a month: usually under a minute or two.
- A large portfolio over the full 62-day window: a few minutes, sometimes up to ten on the busiest accounts.

While a run is in progress, leave the tab open. You can keep working elsewhere on your computer; just don't close the browser or navigate this tab to another PX page. An elapsed-time card shows progress, and your results appear on the same page when it finishes.
